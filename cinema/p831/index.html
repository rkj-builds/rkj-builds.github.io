Overview

How might we let a venue take its own bookings, so one caretaker can run the desk without doing sums?

Context

In the first months, a booking was a conversation. Somebody called or messaged the venue's number, and a person checked a paper sheet, quoted a price from memory, held a slot and remembered to collect. That works at ten bookings a week and collapses at fifty, worst in the evening, when the person on the phone is also the person running the desk.

Problem

72 of 85 bookings in May needed a person

At about ten minutes of calls and messages each, that was around 12 hours a month on bookings alone, plus "did anyone pay cash today?" most days. It meant keeping staff on the phone, and that came straight out of revenue.

The choice

No app. The chat does the work.

In Jamshedpur, people already message a business on WhatsApp, so instead of moving them to an app, I made the chat do the work: book in a form inside the chat, pay the deposit by UPI without leaving it, get a reminder the evening before, cancel and keep the money as credit, and ask about prices in Hindi or English.

Solution

One system behind every way in

  • WhatsApp bookingA form inside the chat, UPI in the chat, a reminder the night before. Parents renew their child's club in two taps and the invoice arrives by itself.
  • One availability moduleThe website, the desk, the bot and a phone voice agent all ask one place what's free, and it runs again on the server when a booking is written. A race for the last court is a refusal, never a double booking.
  • A desk for the ownerCourts across, time down, every booking a block with a name and an amount. Adding a booking asks for a time, not a court.
  • Staff ModeTwo taps, Start and Finish. Overtime follows one published rule, and the screen says one number: "Take this money: ₹175". In Hindi since 21 October, for my dad at the desk.
Iterations

Where the data said otherwise

  • Per-minute billingExact, and impossible to defend at the counter. Now: ten free minutes, then fifteen-minute steps, published on the bill.
  • ₹11,996 vs ₹5,998A reminder counted register rows; one parent had two children. Caught in review. Every amount in a message is now checked against the register first.
  • "8.5:00 – 10.5:00 PM"The maths was right and the message read like a bug. Every length of time now goes through one formatter.
  • The site went downNothing had been deployed: the free database tier ran out of its disk budget. Now capacity is ruled out before blaming a deploy.
Teamwork

Built with a co-founder, a caretaker and Claude Code

My co-founder runs the venue; my dad runs the desk. 1,698 commits in 208 days, 70% of them co-authored with Claude Code. I write the spec and review every diff. Each failure gets a short write-up the week it happens.
