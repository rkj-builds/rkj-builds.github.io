The tickets came with the answer already drawn

A YWEB ticket arrives as a design ask with references attached: a competitor's page, three wireframes, a HubSpot form. Draw what's attached and the team gets what it expected, whether or not it moves anything.

Meanwhile the numbers were moving on their own. On Build My Quote, Continue to Extras fell from about 29% to 23% of destination users while traffic rose 11%. It's the page behind bookings worth about $12k each.

Build My Quote (YWEB-1830) proved a better shape, but I put that case study together by hand, and every new session began with me re-explaining the role. So on 30 September I wrote the method down, once, where both the team and the tooling can read it.

Four rules that don't change from ticket to ticket

1Metric first

One primary metric, up to three secondary and up to five guardrails, each with its baseline, all named before any design.

2Evidence over intuition

An existing page uses its own data. A new page uses the closest analogue, site-wide baselines, past tests, competitors and benchmarks. Raw data is stored first.

3Challenge the brief

The Challenge block is required on every ticket, even when it ends up agreeing with the brief. Push back with a cite, not an opinion.

4A case study every time

Sized to the ticket and designed to be skimmed, plus a log entry so the next ticket starts from this one.

Eleven steps. It stops for me at six.

The skill runs the steps that are work and stops at the ones that are judgement. It never draws: design goes to /helix-explore, and promotion goes to /helix-exploration-promote.

  1. IntakeTicket, linked tickets, comments and attachment text copied word for word. Prints "Starting from" with the matching log entries.
  2. SizeS, M or L, with one line of reasoning.
  3. MetricPrimary, secondary and guardrails, picked from the metric tree.
  4. EvidenceRaw pulls into data/*-raw.md, then the evidence index.
  5. HypothesesA fixed shape for each, a principle and two ethics questions, then the Challenge block.
  6. DesignOne hypothesis per variant, then the review checklist and the states pass.
  7. InstrumentationEvent, trigger, params, exists or needed, owner. Hidden attribution fields.
  8. Measurement planA/B or pre/post, sample per arm, read date, stop rules.
  9. ShipThe ticket comment, approved. Then the push and PR, approved. Never the merge.
  10. Case studyScaffolded at intake and served on the branch preview with no login.
  11. Readout and logThe result with denominators, by device, and what carries forward.
  12. The log entry is the first thing intake reads on the next ticket.

Looker (L1a), users and events, one scale. Continue was about 29% before traffic rose 11%. By device: mobile 21%, desktop 30%.

The tree goes from users to Continue to purchase. Each ticket picks one primary metric from it and guards the steps either side. The paid page's leads hang off the same tree, so a lead form can't claim a win that loses bookings.

BVI page, mobile taps, 30 days to 30 September 2026

Clarity heatmap (C2), taps rather than users. The page the paid ads already land on.

Photos first, phone field last. The wireframe put the form over the photo and asked for a phone number first. The evidence says the photo is what people do first, and the phone number is what they almost never touch.

Ethics question 1Would the visitor agree if they saw how it was built?
Ethics question 2Does it still work if they know?

If a hypothesis fails either question, it's a dark pattern: it gets dropped, and the Challenge block says why. These are named and refused:

  • pre-ticked consent or upsells
  • counts on cached availability
  • countdowns that reset
  • confirmshaming
  • costs revealed at payment
  • a hard-to-find cancel
  • forced continuity
  • Zeigarnik nagging
  • a phone number only for sales calls

The folder every ticket gets

new-case-study.py YWEB-1708 paid-hero --size M creates, in one command:

  • case-study.page.html: the designed page people see
  • case-study.md: the archive, where every number has a denominator, a date range and a cite
  • data/jira-raw.md, clarity-raw.md, looker-raw.md, …: verbatim pulls
  • evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.* and evidence-index.md: figure, source, claim, boundary
  • instrumentation.md, measurement-plan.md, README.md (the decision log)
  • src/explorations/<slug>/case-study/page.tsx: the page on the branch preview, no login
Tracking limits known before any test
  • GA4 productInteraction records the control, not the value or element id. Instrumentation comes before any BMQ test.
  • No Clarity funnel is configured for BMQ.
  • GTM tag 1793 (Microsoft Ads purchase) breaks on cartValue.
  • BMQ availability is cached, so scarcity copy uses bands, never counts.
