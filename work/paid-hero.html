Ask about the trip before the person

A yacht charter brand needed a hero with a lead form for its paid ads. The evidence put four ideas into it. We shipped the smallest version first, as the control, so each idea gets a test of its own.

Sep–Oct 2026MineThree variants, one finalVersion 1 shipped 14 Oct, tests next

What the evidence changed in the brief

The brief: a hero with an embedded form, above the fold, modelled on another brand's paid page. The wireframes asked for name, email and phone first.

  • Trip questions first. Every comparable form asks where, when and how many before who. Contact details moved to a second step.
  • Photos first on a phone. 81.5% of paid sessions were phones, and paging the gallery was 29.9% of mobile taps on the page these ads already land on.
  • Price in the path. The reference page hides price. This brand shows one, and shared quotes were 57% of the leads sales qualified that week.

Three hypotheses, three variants

One hypothesis per variant, so a test could say which idea did the work.

One hero for any destination

The team asked for one final. It folds the three together: the photo-led layout on a phone, the split hero on desktop, the price as a toggle per page. Editors set the destination; the same component renders it.

Every state, then the system

Nothing validated in the first build. I designed each state as a decision: an error shows when you leave a field, clears on the keystroke that fixes it, and says what to enter. A failed send keeps every answer.

Then the final became design-system components: Hero, LeadForm, MediaCarousel, StickyBar, StatList. A review against the approved design made seven more changes, from the dialog's look to the button's words.

Version 1 is the control

A campaign might run a generic ad with no destination, so the form learned to ask for one first.

And four ideas launched together would leave no way to know which one earned the result. So version 1 is the smallest page that can take a paid click. The rest became a sequence, one variable each.

  • Test 1. The proof strip under the hero: the strongest pattern signal and the cheapest addition.
  • Test 2. The shorter phone form: chips and one button inside the fold, details in a sheet.
  • Later. Price in the path, once the departures data is wired.

Ship the smallest page that can take a paid click. Every idea after it gets a real baseline.
