Half an hour is thirty minutes

A reminder went out saying "8.5:00 – 10.5:00 PM". The arithmetic was right. The message was wrong.

Where
Booking reminders, the WhatsApp flow, membership balances
Cost
A reminder that read like a bug
The rule
A length of time a person reads is spelled out, never divided. One formatter, no inline maths.

What happened

Somewhere a duration was minutes divided by sixty, and the result was printed. Rahul: "I don't want the time to be treated as 0.5. That's stupid to assume that half an hour is time."

What it taught us

A decimal hour reads as a bug even when it is correct. Half an hour is thirty minutes to the person reading it. A balance of court-hours is a duration too, and gets the same treatment.

The rule now

"1 hour 30 minutes" in anything a customer reads, "1 hr 30 min" in a tight dashboard spot, never "1.5 hours". Nobody writes minutes divided by sixty inline; there is a shared formatter. Three copies of it exist today, left alone on purpose rather than churning a seven-thousand-line page for no defect, and nobody is allowed to write a fourth. A statistical average, like hours a day on the revenue page, is not a duration and is left alone.
