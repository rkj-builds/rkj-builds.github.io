Names stay on the register until someone stops them

A membership ended on the calendar and the child vanished from the attendance sheet. The parent had not paid for the next month yet. Nobody was going to ask them now.

Where
The kids' programme, the attendance register
Cost
Names disappearing before anyone could collect
The rule
The desk records money in arrears. Only four things take a name off a register, and the end of a month is not one of them.

What happened

The register was built as if everything happened in real time: a month ends, the name goes. The desk does not work like that. Attendance from the 8th, 15th and 22nd is typed in on the 24th. Payment arrives when it arrives. Rahul: "once a membership is added, unless I cancel it manually, don't remove that name... I will update the payment once it is received. You can obviously nudge me, but don't remove it."

What it taught us

A name that vanishes when a month ends vanishes before anyone could collect. Every forward-only assumption in a desk tool costs money: date pickers with a minimum of today, session lists filtered to upcoming, rosters that drop the lapsed.

The rule now

A lapsed member stays on the register flagged "New month · unpaid". A member who has used up their sessions stays on, flagged. Only four things remove a name: a cancelled entry, a stop date, a refund that ended the month, or a later month for the same child. And nobody is ever told to cancel a paid month, because cancelling pulls a delivered payment out of the settlements.
